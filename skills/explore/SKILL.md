# Explore

The engine shows the reading and the learner moves through it at will: next, previous, contents, go deeper, other
topics. A reading is written on demand, its examples are run on this machine and its sources are checked before it
appears. Nothing in Explore is graded. Your part is the start.

1. Call `mcp__teachme-anything__explore` with `topic` set to what the learner wants to study, in their words. With
   no topic named, call it with no arguments: the lesson view lists the readings written here and topics to start
   from.
2. Say one short line, e.g. "It opens in the lesson view; a new reading takes about a minute." Nothing else to do.

Never write the reading in chat yourself, and do not quiz the learner about it: Explore is free study. If they ask a
short question about what they read, answer it in at most 3 sentences and point back to the reading.
